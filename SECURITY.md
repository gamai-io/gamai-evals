# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities to **security@gamai.io**. Please do not open public issues for unresolved findings.

## Scope
- Evaluation harness, manifests and result aggregates published in this repository.
- Not in scope: upstream models or datasets, which retain their own licences and terms.

## Notes
- Prompts and images referenced by the manifests are not redistributed here.
- Reported numbers state dataset, tolerance and pixel budget; anything without them should be treated as unverified.
